# 🌸 Introduction

Sharosa Atelier is a creative portfolio website designed to showcase a diverse collection of artistic works through a structured and visually engaging digital platform.

The website serves as a personal creative gallery containing traditional Indian art, mandala artwork, sketches, paper crafts, architectural drawings, decorative creations, and best-out-of-waste projects.

Unlike conventional portfolios that simply display images, Sharosa Atelier aims to create a curated experience where visitors can explore different forms of creativity through organized categories and an elegant user interface.

The project combines artistic storytelling with modern web development principles to create a platform that is both aesthetically pleasing and technically efficient.

# 🌹 About the Name

The name **Sharosa Atelier** was carefully crafted to represent both my personality and artistic vision.

### Meaning Breakdown

| Component | Meaning |
|------------|----------|
| Sha | Derived from my name, Sharvi |
| Rosa | Spanish word for Pink |
| Atelier | French term for an Artist's Studio |

### Why "Rosa"?

Pink has always been a color I associate with creativity, elegance, warmth, and artistic expression.

### Why "Atelier"?

Unlike a simple gallery, an atelier is a space where ideas are conceived, developed, and transformed into meaningful creations.

Therefore, Sharosa Atelier symbolizes:

> "Sharvi's Creative Studio where ideas bloom into art."

# ❓ Problem Statement

Artists often share their work through scattered social media posts, making it difficult to present their complete creative journey in a structured manner.

Challenges include:

- Lack of centralized presentation
- Limited categorization
- Inconsistent visual identity
- Difficulty showcasing diverse art forms together

Sharosa Atelier addresses these challenges by providing a unified and organized digital portfolio.
